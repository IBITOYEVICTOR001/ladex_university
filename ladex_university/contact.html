Privacy

When you use the contact form or apply online, we collect the details you submit, such as your name, email address, phone number and application information, so that we can reply to you and process applications. This information is stored with Google Firebase and is available only to authorised university staff and, for applications, to the applicant. To ask for your information to be corrected or removed, contact us using the details on this page.

Terms

Information on this website, including programmes, fees and dates, is provided for general guidance and may change. Submitting an application does not guarantee admission. Admission decisions are made by the university after review.

Cookies

This website does not use marketing or analytics cookies. Signing in to the student portal or the staff area uses Google Firebase Authentication, which keeps a sign-in session in your browser. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.
