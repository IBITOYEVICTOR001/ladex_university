About Ladex

Our story

Built around the possibility in every learner.

Ladex International University is a fictional institution imagined in 2011 by educators and civic leaders who believed a world-class Nigerian university should be both rigorous and deeply connected to its community.

Today, our story is one of curious minds, practical research and graduates who lead with confidence and care.

What guides us

Purpose with principle.

Our mission

To develop capable, ethical graduates through teaching, research and service that respond to Africa's opportunities.

Our vision

To be a trusted international university where ideas become useful progress.

Our values

Integrity, curiosity, inclusion, courage and generous collaboration guide every decision.

Accreditation & approvals

Institutional standing

For this fictional demonstration, Ladex is presented as approved by the National Universities Commission (NUC) and its programmes as subject to relevant professional-body accreditation. Always verify approval directly with the applicable regulator before making an education decision.
