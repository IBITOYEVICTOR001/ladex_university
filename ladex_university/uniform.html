Dress code & supplies

Dress with purpose

Professional, practical, respectful.

Everyday learning

Wear neat, modest clothing suitable for classes, laboratories and shared learning spaces. Keep your student identity card visible on campus.

Formal occasions

For presentations and official events, choose smart business attire in navy, white, black or neutral tones.

Specialist safety

Laboratory coats, closed shoes and other protective equipment are compulsory wherever departmental signs require them.

Study essentials

Required textbooks & stationery

Core textbook guidance

  • Current course reading lists, issued by each department
  • Academic writing and referencing guide
  • Faculty-specific laboratory or studio manuals
  • One approved scientific calculator for relevant programmes

Stationery checklist

  • Notebooks or refill pads for each course
  • Blue or black pens, pencils and eraser
  • Folder for certificates and submitted work
  • USB drive or secure cloud-storage access
