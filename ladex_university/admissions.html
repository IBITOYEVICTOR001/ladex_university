Entry requirements

What you need to apply.

UTME score

A JAMB score of 180 or above is required for undergraduate consideration.

O'level results

At least five credits, including English Language and Mathematics, in no more than two sittings.

Documents

Upload your result slips, birth record and a recent passport photograph during your application.
